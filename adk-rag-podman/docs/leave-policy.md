# Acme Corp Leave Policy (sample data)

## Annual leave
Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave accrues at 2 days per month.
Up to 10 unused days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above that lapses on 31 March.

## Sick leave
Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

## Work from home
Employees may work from home up to 2 days per week with manager approval. Requests are raised in the HR portal at least one day in advance.
